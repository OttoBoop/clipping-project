## Interpretation

The second pass strengthens three conclusions:

1. **Political monitoring is a real category.** Political Brain, MonitoraBR, and
   Conectare explicitly address mandates, campaigns, candidates, parties, or
   vereadores.
2. **Most competitors bundle clipping with broader intelligence.** Crisis
   alerts, adversary monitoring, content generation, BI, social/media analysis,
   and reports are common claims.
3. **Otavio's V1 should sell focus and affordability, not breadth.** The first
   offer should be a scoped private dashboard plus operated updates and a short
   weekly summary. Competitor-style extras should be add-ons.

## V1 Positioning Update

Use this language:

```text
clipping privado e monitorado para gabinete pequeno
painel segregado por senha
noticias agrupadas por alvo/tema
duas atualizacoes por semana
resumo semanal curto
sem misturar dados de outros clientes
```

Avoid:

```text
inteligencia politica completa
monitoramento total de adversarios
crisis room
social listening completo
relatorio longo diario
BI/ROI/AVE completo
```
