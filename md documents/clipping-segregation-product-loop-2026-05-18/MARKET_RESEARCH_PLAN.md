# Market Research Plan - Political Clipping Product

_Created 2026-05-18 by Atlas/Codex._

This is a research plan, not completed market research.

Do not invent prices or competitor claims. When executing this plan, use current
sources and record links/dates.

## Research Goal

Find whether a private, segmented clipping dashboard for politicians/advisors
can be sold at a price that helps Otavio pay for AI tooling, hosting, and
maintenance.

## Buyer Hypotheses

Possible buyers:

- municipal politicians;
- chiefs of staff;
- press advisors;
- campaign consultants;
- small communications agencies serving political clients;
- issue-based organizations that monitor local agenda.

## Core Questions

- Who already sells media monitoring/clipping to politicians in Brazil?
- What do small political offices pay for clipping or press monitoring?
- Are competitors selling dashboards, PDFs, WhatsApp alerts, analyst summaries,
  or raw mention tracking?
- Does the buyer care more about mentions, grouped narratives, sentiment, topic
  agenda, or proof for social media?
- How frequently do they expect updates?
- What level of manual review is acceptable?
- What price covers Otavio's AI/tooling cost without creating unsustainable
  bespoke work?

## Competitor Categories To Search

- clipping agencies;
- media monitoring platforms;
- political communications consultancies;
- social listening tools;
- press advisory packages;
- local political intelligence products.

## Evidence To Capture

For each competitor or comparable offer:

- name;
- URL;
- target customer;
- features;
- pricing if public;
- whether pricing is quote-based;
- dashboard/export/alert format;
- evidence date;
- notes on how Otavio's product can be simpler or cheaper.

## Interview Questions

If talking to a possible buyer:

- How do you currently know when you or your issue appears in the press?
- What is annoying about your current clipping process?
- How fast do you need to know?
- Would a private dashboard be useful, or do you prefer WhatsApp/PDF?
- What would make you trust the results?
- What would make this not worth paying for?

## Do Not Research Yet If

- production segregation is not verified;
- demo data leaks another profile;
- no clear demo profile exists;
- the app still exposes operator controls to viewers.

## Output Of The First Research Pass

Create a short table with:

```text
Competitor | Buyer | Offer | Pricing evidence | Delivery format | Notes
```

Then update `FIRST_SELLABLE_PACKAGE.md` with pricing/package implications.
